Privacy notice

Updated September 22, 2026. Maintainer: kjhq. This notice covers the Google Drive save functionality of this development fork.

Data accessed and why

Google sign-in supplies your stable account identifier, email address, and basic profile information so the launcher can identify the connected account. The launcher requests drive.file: access to files created by the app or explicitly made available to it. It does not request access to your entire Drive.

Selected game saves are compressed and uploaded with metadata including game/save identity, snapshot ancestry, timestamps, device identifier and name, file hashes, path mappings, and save-format details. Save files and paths can contain personal information. Choose custom save paths carefully.

Where data goes

Save transfers go directly from the launcher to Google's APIs over HTTPS. Backups are stored in your Google Drive. This fork does not operate a separate server that receives your Google tokens or save archives. The maintainer cannot retrieve your Drive saves through the project's public source repository.

Google account data is used to provide account connection, backup, restore, synchronization, and history. It is not sold, used for advertising, or used to train AI models. Waypoint's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Local storage and protection

The launcher stores encrypted Google credentials using Electron safeStorage backed by the operating system. When secure encryption is unavailable on Linux, connection is session-only. Tokens remain in the main process. Local queued backups, restore journals, account-specific sync state, and save-path selections are stored in the application's user-data directory. Backup archives are not additionally encrypted by this application; Google and your device provide their respective storage protections.

Retention and deletion

The launcher keeps the newest ten snapshot payloads per save identity, plus unresolved branch heads and pinned snapshots. Older payloads are moved to Drive Trash. Commit metadata remains for ancestry. Google applies its own Trash retention rules.

Disconnect removes local credentials and cancels transfers. It does not delete cloud backups or pending local backups. You can revoke access in Google Account connections and delete app files in Drive. Deleting cloud backups in the launcher does not delete your live game saves. Removing the separate Waypoint user-data directory after closing the launcher removes its local queues and cached state; preserve any data you still need first.

Other launcher services and this website

Catalogue, artwork, manifests, downloads, account features, and the inherited WorkWonders help/changelog integration may contact their respective providers. They are separate from Google save storage. Their normal requests can include network information such as IP address and any data you choose to provide to those services. This fork's release configuration does not enable a Sentry reporting destination by default.

This documentation website is hosted by GitHub Pages and uses no analytics scripts or advertising cookies. GitHub may process request information under its own privacy policy. Links to third-party services have their own policies.

Contact and support

For privacy questions, contact arvintro@gmail.com. For ordinary bugs, use the fork issue tracker. Do not post passwords, tokens, private save files, or unredacted paths in public issues. The maintainer cannot delete copies stored in your Google account; use the controls described above.
